# The Penyamun

The Penyamun are the shadow network of [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md). They have dens of agents in [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md) and the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md), their influence felt mostly in the western half of Nuitai. They are operators of stealth for hire. Spies, assassins and thieves. If you need something dirty done quietly, you have the coin, but don't have the morals, you contact the Penyamun. They steal secrets and goods for clients, smuggle information and contraband (if small) between ports, carry out quiet “removals” when a client needs someone gone, and maintain an invisible influence over city politics through bribery, blackmail, and espionage. As there is some overlap in their domains, mostly to do with smuggling and thievery, Penyamun have been known from time to time to clash with the lanun of Nuitai, the pirates of the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md). While the Penyamun carry out their orders quietly, professionally, in an organized structure, pirates are a loud and rowdy that answer to no one and take what they want on the seas by cannon fire. Due to their agility, the Vanara variety of the [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) people are common among their ranks.
