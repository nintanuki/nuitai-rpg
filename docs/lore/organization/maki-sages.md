# Maki Sages

The Maki Sages are a group of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) worshipers who broke off from the [Priests of Māra](../organization/the-nature-priests-of-māra-iti.md) a century ago when they integrated [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md) , later formalized in [Māra](../organization/māra.md) doctrine as the worship of [Ra Lanu](../person/ra-lanu.md), the Akua Iti of sunlight, which the Sages saw as an encroachment upon [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) ’s traditional domain of sunlight. To the Sages, Ra as [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) is a reflection of mortal misunderstanding. They believe the divine should not be redefined by foreign tongues.

They are hermits who can be found deeper in the more dangerous parts of the forests of [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md). They differentiate themselves by donning a purple spiral on their green robes, the symbol of [Mana](../law/mana.md), and unlike the pacifistic priests of Māra have mastered martial spell-casting and the use of nature magic for self defense as well as healing, a necessity if one is to live among the more unforgiving of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) ’s children. The Maki Sages still dance and spin using similar forms, but with slightly more speed that begins with softness and develops into force.
