# The Callers

The Makua Callers are the shamans of the [Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md) , wielders of dark Mana who commune with the spirits of the dead. Clad in robes of black and purple adorned with bones and skulls, they are both revered and feared by their people.

Unlike necromancers, the Callers do not enslave the dead. The spirits they summon come willingly, often ancestors of the Callers themselves, though not always. This communion is built on respect, a sacred dialogue between the living and those who have passed into [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md) ’s domain. A relationship must be built with the dead. However, such communion carries grave danger. Malevolent entities may impersonate spirits, or the dead themselves may return wrathful. Because of this, only the wisest and eldest Callers are permitted to work in solitude.

This practice of "voluntary necromancy" is not considered taboo in the Makua Archipelago, in fact the Callers have been known to provide services. Funerary rites, corpse preparation, comforting the grieving, and helping the grieving contact lost loved ones for one last goodbye. Some callers, for the price, contact the dead for information, this has even been used as a form of investigation. Be wary if you plan to commit the sinful act of cold blooded murder in this archipelago, your victim just might tell on you.

Though they do not worship [Akua](../law/akua.md) in the traditional sense, the Callers pay homage to Moanaku, the Akua of Death, who rules both sea and underworld, and is the patron saint of secrets and the dark arts. Many among the callers also acknowledge the teachings of Anúa, although they follow older traditions, and while they are not hostile to [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) they have completely rejected the teachings of Shaka, which claims souls are reborn after death, and the status of one's rebirth depends on the deeds and purity of the soul in a previous life. The Callers eschew such moralized cosmology.

In battle, Callers can channel Mana not only to summon spirits for aid, but to hurl lightning or unleash curses, a dangerous art that can consume even the wielder if misused.
