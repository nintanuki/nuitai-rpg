# Māra

Māra is one of the most widely practiced spiritual traditions among the [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md) peoples of the eastern seas. While the practice of both Anua and Shaka worship in paralell are common, Mara attempts to make the fusion explicit in its doctrine.

Mara originated on the Māra Iti Islands (hence the name) through the teachings of the Priests of Māra, who sought to reconcile their native animist faith, [Anúa](../organization/anúa.md), and their devotion to [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) with the doctrines of light and recurrence brought by [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) missionaries devoted to the [Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md). The resulting synthesis regards all natural life as sacred while also embracing the purifying and illuminating aspects of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md)’s light. One can be reborn into Shaka's [light world](../location/the-light-realm.md) by devoting oneself to not just his teachings, but also Lanu and her creations in this world. Some interpretations consider Shaka to be an Akua Iti or aspect of Lanu as her light, a re-interpretation of the native sun god [Ra Lanu](../person/ra-lanu.md).

Māra spread from [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) across the [The Lanuroa Archipelago](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md) and is more prominent in the northeast of Nuitai, becoming less common further south.

The practice varies by island but retains the same symbolism. Priests wear robes of green and gold, dance beneath forest canopies, and recite prayers facing both the rising sun and bowing to the forests. Offerings of fruit, woven garlands, and clean water are made to honor Lanu’s gentle breeze and Shaka’s warm light. Though rooted in pacifism, Māra is not passive or esoteric. Its followers teach that harmony must be tended like a garden, through patience, humility, and labor.

A minority of traditionalists in Mara Iti, known as the [Maki Sages](../organization/maki-sages.md), reject the inclusion of Shaka into the pantheon of [Akua](../law/akua.md) and continue to devote themselves only to Lanu.
