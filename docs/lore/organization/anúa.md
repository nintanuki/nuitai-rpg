# Anúa

The native animist faith of the people of Nuitai. Anúa teaches that the natural world itself is divine, and that the people and animals that inhabit it are spirits themselves. Everything from the rocks to the trees to the clouds themselves are imbued with [Mana](../law/mana.md) , a spiritual force that permeates the universe, the source of all magic. Anuic beliefs typically involve devotion to one or more [Akua](../law/akua.md), gods that are personifications of the forces of nature.

Within the last century, the teachings of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) has been spread by the foreign [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) throughout Nuitai. At present day most of the native islanders of Nuitai now follow some religious practice that combines traditional Anuic beliefs with the more modern salvationist teachings of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md), which introduced structure moral codes and the idea that actions in this life affect one's reincarnation into the next.
