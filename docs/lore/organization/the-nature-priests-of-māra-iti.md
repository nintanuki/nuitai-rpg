# The Nature Priests of Māra Iti

In [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) there is an old spiritual order known as the Priests of Māra whose temple is the forest itself. They practice [Māra](../organization/māra.md) , a syncretic variation of [Anúa](../organization/anúa.md) , the native faith of the people of Nuitai. Anúa teaches that the natural world itself is divine, and that the people and animals that inhabit it are spirits themselves. Everything from the rocks to the trees to the clouds themselves are imbued with [Mana](../law/mana.md), a spiritual force that permeates the universe, the source of all magic.

The Priests of Māra bow to the world and to themselves, and practice slow and gentle, sacred ritual dances, marked by gestures such as spinning in circles and raising one’s arms from the ground to the sky, to encourage the growth of crops and the healing of wounds. They don green and yellow robes to honor the sun and the forests, and white to honor the sky.

The Priests of Māra once revered only [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) , an [Akua](../law/akua.md) whose domain is the sun, the sky and the forests. When the [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) missionaries arrived and spread the teachings of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) , the people of Māra Iti were receptive, they did not believe this religion that spoke of the purifying power of the sun’s light, the cycle of death and rebirth, and the interconnectedness of all things, contradicted their Anuic beliefs. It helped that the missionaries did not belittle or disrespect their native beliefs, in fact they seemed quite curious about them. The result was Māra a spiritual practice that synergized the beliefs of Anua and worship of Lanu with the teachings of Shaka, who they accepted as [Ra](../person/ra-lanu.md) , an Akua Iti born of Lanu’s light, integrating his teachings into their veneration of the natural world.. They donned the crest of Shaka’s sun but still danced in the forest.

However, some priests rejected the identification of Shaka as Ra, seeing it as a foreign incursion and a corruption of Lanu’s light, and broke off into a separate sect known as the [Maki Sages](../organization/maki-sages.md).
