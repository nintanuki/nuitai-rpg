# The Floating Continent

Hanging high above the maelstrom, in the sky, is probably the most incredible site in all of Nuitai; a floating continent. No one knows where it came from and no one has ever been able to reach it. It has been theorized that the continent rose out of the ocean and caused the maelstrom. The floating continent can be seen from anywhere in Nuitai and is used as a convenient reference point for sailors and navigators.
