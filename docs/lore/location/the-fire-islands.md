# The Fire Islands

To the southeast of the great maelstrom are the fire islands. While volcanoes can be found all over the islands of Nuitai, they are the most numerous and most active here than anywhere else. The skies above these islands are filled with steam and ash as rivers of magma flow eternally from the mountains into the oceans. Salamanders, lava dragons, fire spirits and other wildlife that are either borne of or resistant to fire call these islands their home.

As do the fiery Ahi’Mana Fire Dancers, followers of the Goddess [Mauga](../person/mauga.md), mistress of volcanoes. The fire dancers are a force to be reckoned with. They specialize in destruction, through both martial prowess and powerful fire magic, they can break through any defense. Only the foolish do not fear their wrath. Despite their terrifyingly destructive abilities, their movements are complex and beautiful. They are practitioners of [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md), a dance based martial art inspired by the mesmerizing unpredictable movements of fire.

Deep within the jungles of the islands of this flaming archipelago live also the famous Hunters of Nuitai. Masters of archery, spear fishing and knowledge of survival in the wild. They are known to light their weapons on fire and hide in green foliage using war paint as camouflage. Experts in both killing and healing, their patron deities are [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) and Mauga, goddesses of the forest and creation. They take life but also respect it.

Travelers to the fire islands should take heed of the many dangers, from both the local wildlife and the locals themselves. This corner of Nuitai is not for the faint of heart
