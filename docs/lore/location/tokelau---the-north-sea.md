# Tokelau - The North Sea

To the north of the great maelstrom is arguably the most dangerous region of Nuitai to sail. Frigid cold, constant violent storms, and the largest sea monsters that rival the size of those outside the islands bordering the maelstrom. Islands in these waters are few and far between, small and inhospitable. No one lives here, and it is debatable whether it is safer to sail these seas to travel from the Kame Kingdom to Māra Iti or to ride the maelstrom itself.
