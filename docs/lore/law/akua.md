# Akua

The Akua are the gods of Nuitai. They are personifications or great spirits that embody aspects of [Mana](../law/mana.md). The three primary Akua Nui that traditionally represent the head of the Nuitai pantheon are [Lanu](../person/lanu.md), the goddess of life and nature, [Mauga](../person/mauga.md), the goddess of fire and change, and [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md), the god of the sea and death.

In the world of Nuitai, there are beings regarded as being halfway between the three great Akua Nui and mortals. They are known as the Akua Iti. Sometimes referred to as “demi-gods,” they each embody an aspect or “piece” of a greater Akua Nui. [Hau](../person/hau.md) is the wind, [Makei](../person/makei.md) is the transition from life to death, [Ra](../person/ra-lanu.md) is the sun and it's light.

Akua Iti can take human form and interact with mortals, they have magic powers and can pop in and out of existence. They are not stable, some walking the world for centuries before splitting or merging with other Akua Iti. Some are merged aspects of two or all three of the Akua Nui.

The [Kame](../species/kame.md) have their own pantheon of Akua which they refer to as the Kamekami.
