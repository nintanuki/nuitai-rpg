# Sailing

The player begins in the [Māra Iti Islands](../location/māra-iti-islands.md), and can sail in either a clockwise or counterclockwise direction. Counterclockwise takes longer than clockwise travel (penalty for backtracking), and every trip requires provisions and faces danger. As players advance through the story they will get various ship upgrades. Two upgrades that make ocean travel safer and faster, and a final airship upgrade that can avoid the maelstrom entirely, and provide access to floating islands.

[The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md) at the center of the map spins faster at the core where it is more dangerous but ships can pick up more speed. The further out from the core of the maelstrom you go, the less intense the current and weather get, and the benefits and penalties of sailing in ether direction are less exaggerated.

Nuitai is about 10,000 miles wide (further out there is no wind and giant predatory sea monsters that make seas too far out from the maelstrom impossible to explore) so any point in Nuitai is about 5000 miles from the core of the maelstrom.

The effects of the maelstrom are gradient however it is normally divided into six zones.

### Zones of the Great Maelstrom

- Outer Sea (“The Calm Ring”): 3,500 – 5,000 miles from the core. Gentle tides and predictable winds. Navigation easy. Only faint hints of the Maelstrom’s pull. Ideal for trade and island hopping. Clockwise travel is roughly 1.10x the speed, counterclockwise travel is .090x the speed. Effects are barely noticeable for short distances, but cumulative for long voyages.

- The Driftbelt (“Mariner’s Drift”): 2,500 – 3,500 miles from the core. Constant, steady clockwise drift. Occasional rough weather but safe for most ships (not counting the freak unpredictable storms that are common in Nuitai, independent of the maelstrom). Clockwise travel is roughly 1.25x the speed, counterclockwise travel is 0.75x the speed.

- The Surge Zone (“Sailor’s Run”): 1,500 – 2,500 miles from the core. Strong currents give daring captains incredible speed. The waves grow taller, and navigation requires experience. A favorite test for competitive sailors. Clockwise travel is roughly 1.5x the speed, counterclockwise travel is 0.5x the speed.

- The Tempest Ring (“Storm’s Edge”): 1,000 – 1,500 miles from the core. Furious winds, towering waves, and constant lightning. Only legendary ships or those blessed by Moanaku survive long here. Speed doubles, but one wrong turn means disaster. Clockwise travel is roughly twice the speed, counterclockwise travel is ×0.25 the speed.

- The Eye’s Reach (“The Abyssal Spiral”): 500 – 1,000 miles from the core. Gravity and magic twist the sea itself. Time and space distort. Ships caught here are either lost forever or reappear months later in strange places. The only sailors that venture here are those with a death wish.

- The Core (“The Heart of Moanaku”): 0 – 500 miles from the center. No ship has ever returned. This is certain death.

- [The Lanuroa Archipelago](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md) - Zone 1: Calm, safe, navigable seas.

- [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md) - Bordering Zones 1 & 2: Mostly calm seas with localized hazards.

- [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md) - Zone 2: Dark, stormy seas.

- [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md) - Bordering Zones 1 & 2: Mostly calm seas with localized hazards.

- [Kota Emas - Bandari City](../settlement/kota-emas.md) - Zone 1: Calm, safe, navigable seas.

- [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) - Zone 1: Calm, safe, navigable seas.

- [Tokelau - The North Sea](../location/tokelau---the-north-sea.md) - Technically in Zones 1 and 2, but deadly no matter the zone: even if it’s far from the Maelstrom, the seas are icy, stormy, and full of giant monsters. Any travel along the north of the Maelstrom is a huge risk.
