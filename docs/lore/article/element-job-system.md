# Element Job System

## Element System

There are six elements, each with a primary battle role:

- **Ahi (Fire)** - Fighter: Deal physical damage using martial arts.

- **Lau (Nature)** - Healer: Restore health and cure negative status effects.

- ** ****Wai (Water)** - Defender: Raise defense and take damage in place of allies.

- ** Ra (Light)** - Enhancer: Improve allies’ abilities and strength (buffs)

- **Aku (Shadow)** - Saboteur: Cause negative status effects (debuffs)

- ** ****Mana (Spirit)**** **- Blaster: Deal magical damage using spells.

Every playable character or class has a combination of two elemental affinities that defines their playstyle, meaning every character fights with a synergy of two strategies. For example; characters with **Ahi **or **Wai **are excellent at both attacking and defending, characters with both **Lau **and **Aku **both heal and inflict ailments, etc. Some classes can augment abilities of one type with the element of another. For example, while **Lau **is defined as a physical element normally used for healing, a class that combines **Mana **with **Lau **can do **Lau **elemental magic damage using offensive **Mana **Spells.

### Elements Strengths and Weaknesses

- **Ahi **burns **Lau**

- **Lau **drinks **Wai**

- **Wai **quenches **Ahi****.**

- **Ra **purifies **Aku**

- **Aku **corrupts **Mana**

- **Mana **transcends **Ra**.

### Connection to the story and characters

The elements also have thematic flavor. Characters with **Ahi **or **Wai **affinities are typically martial artists, the latter being more patient and less aggressive in temperament than the former. Characters with **Lau **are healers, medicinal or magical, and tend to gravitate toward [Anuic](../organization/anúa.md) beliefs or a connection to nature. Characters with **Ra **are normally either [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) in ancestry or followers of [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md). Characters with **Aku **affinities have a morally grey world view, and characters with **Mana **are magic users with a bias or devotion toward one member of any pantheon of gods. For example, a [Māra](../organization/māra.md) priestess has a dual elemental affinity of **Ra** and **Lau**, reflecting a syncretic faith reconciling the native animist beliefs of east Nuitai with Shakan moral philosophy. However none of these descriptions are strict rules, they are just symbolism or guiding themes, and the element system itself is not woven into the lore nor is it ever mentioned in the story by the characters themselves.

## D&D Character Classes

Because I don't have this system fully worked out yet for it's own game, I decided to try adapting this for preexisting TTRPGs first, starting with the most popular system: Dungeons & Dragons.

My strategy is to ask players to choose two out of the six elements to use as a guideline for their character and class, then the player may choose a class from the Player's Handbook that matches that theme. There will be some restrictins. Because magic in Nuitai is motion based, all magic must have a somatic component, and being unable to move prevents casting. I am also restricting heavy armor and weapons. Because of the culture and nature of magic in Nuitai, there is a natural bias toward monks, clerics or druids. Below is a tentative attempt to match my element combos with existing D&D classes:

### Ahi + Wai

#### D&D Classes

- Monk (Way of the Four Elements or Open Hand)

- Fighter

A talented frontline fighter who balances offense and defense by redirects blows, controlling enemy positioning, and striking explosively.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the "Channeling Mana" section in [Mana](../law/mana.md) as well as the martial arts [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) and [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md).

### Ahi + Lau

#### D&D Classes

- Ranger

- Monk (Way of Mercy)

- Druid (Circle of Wildfire)

A fast, aggressive fighter who mixes offensive pressure with restorative or supportive techniques. Think of a combat medic who knows survival techniques in the wild.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) and the hunters of [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md)

### Ahi + Aku

#### D&D Classes

- Rogue (Assassin)

- Monk (Way of Shadow)

An agile combatant who uses smoke, shadow, or misdirection while delivering sudden debilitating hits. They excel at opening enemies up with dirty tricks and hit-and-run attacks.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [The Penyamun](../organization/the-penyamun.md)

### Ahi + Ra

#### D&D Classes

- Fighter

- Paladin

- Bard (College of Valor)

- Monk (Way of the Kensai or Sun Soul)

A fiery combatant who inspires and empowers allies through dynamic movement and shining displays of Mana. Their strikes are aggressive, but their presence lifts the whole party.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) Zealots of [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md)

### Ahi + Mana

#### D&D Classes

- Bard (College of Swords)

- Monk (Way of the Four Elements)

- Sorcerer (Dragonic Bloodline - Fire)

A martial caster who channels raw spiritual energy into bursts of flame or kinetic power. Expect close-range elemental attacks mixed with explosive spell-like techniques.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) and the dancers of [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md)

### Wai + Lau

#### D&D Classes

- Paladin

- Druid (Circle of the Coast)

- Cleric (Life Domain)

A calm, restorative presence focused on mending wounds, reducing harm, and stabilizing the party. They specialize in consistent healing, cleansing, and protective techniques.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) or [Wai'Ki](../organization/waiki.md) for information about the [Kame](../species/kame.md) guardians of the sacred springs. See also [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md).

### Wai + Aku

#### D&D Classes

- Rogue (Swashbuckler or Arcane Trickster)

- Monk (Shadow)

A defender who relies on dirty tricks and misdirection to protect allies. They prevent danger through confusion, repositioning, and subtle sabotage.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the Lanun (pirates) of the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md)

### Wai + Ra

#### D&D Classes

- Cleric (Light)

- Paladin (Ancients, reskinned to no armor)

- Monk (Sun Soul or Open Hand)

A serene defender who shields allies with flowing movement and radiant empowerment. They focus on prevention: barriers, guidance, and protective auras.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md), the path of the polished mirror in [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md), and the [Archaeological Society of Kota Emas](../organization/archaeological-society-of-kota-emas.md).

### Wai + Mana

#### D&D Classes

- Sorcerer (Sea Origin)

- Fighter

- Wizard (Bladesinging, reflavored as aquatic dance?)

A mana-caster who manipulates water, mist, pressure, and force to control the field. Expect battlefield shaping, soft crowd control, and protective flows.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md) and [Sailing](../article/sailing.md).

### Lau + Aku

#### D&D Classes

- Artificer (Alchemist)

- Druid (Circle of Spores)

- Ranger (Gloom Stalker)

A healer who understands decay as well as growth, debilitating enemies with poison while providing antidotes to allies. Their aid can come with a sting, and their rituals blend both the life and death aspects of nature.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [The Witch Doctors](../organization/the-witch-doctors.md) of [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md)

### Lau + Ra

#### D&D Classes

- Cleric (Light, Life or Nature Domain)

- Druid

- Bard (College of Spirits) (reflavored as ancestral forest-stories?)

A bright, uplifting healer who restores allies while boosting their strength, courage, or clarity. They combine nature’s restoration with supportive blessings and light.

#### Nuitai Lore

[The Nature Priests of Māra Iti](../organization/the-nature-priests-of-māra-iti.md)

### Lau + Mana

#### D&D Classes

- Druid (Circle of the Land – Forest/Grassland)

- Druid (Circle of the Serpent) (homebrew archetype opportunity?)

- Sorcerer

A nature-themed spellcaster who uses spiritual energy to unleash storms of leaves, thorns, winds, or toxins. Less focused on healing, more on shaping nature’s power offensively.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the [Maki Sages](../organization/maki-sages.md) or [Sarpa](../organization/sarpa.md).

### Aku + Ra

#### D&D Classes

- Cleric (Twilight Domain)

- Paladin (Oath of Redemption)

A character who exposes weaknesses, reveals hidden dangers, and manipulates light and shadow to guide the party. They balance uplifting allies with undermining foes.

#### Nuitai Lore

Heretics or apostates of [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md). See also [Cindaku](../species/cindaku.md).

### Aku + Mana

#### D&D Classes

- Warlock (Undying or Great Old One)

- Cleric (Grave Domain)

A conduit to dead or hidden spirits who weakens enemies through curses, hauntings, and shadowy magic. Expect strong debuffs, blasts of arcane lightning spells, spirit interactions, and high-risk power.

#### Nuitai Lore

See [The Callers](../organization/the-callers.md) of [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md)

### Ra + Mana

#### D&D Classes

- Cleric (Light Domain)

- Sorcerer (Divine Soul)

- Wizard (Chronurgy or Divination)

A spellcaster who channels pure light to empower allies and blast foes with focused radiance. They excel at magical buffs, clarity, and offensive holy magic.

#### Nuitai Lore

See the Teachings of Shaka section of [Mana](../law/mana.md).
