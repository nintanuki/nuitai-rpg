# Stone Speech

Throughout the islands of Nuitai, both on the ocean and in the sky, are various ruins and temples left behind by the [Lanigata](../ethnicity/lanigata.md), the ancient sky people, who disappeared three thousand years ago. The player will discover how to perform Po'lelo, or "stone speech", to carve glyphs into the stone and harness the spiritual power of [Mana](../law/mana.md) to imbue life and animation into stone. This can be used to create elevators and moving platforms, open doors, solve puzzles, and create or summon [Pohaku](../species/pohaku.md), stone golems.
