# Bandari

The Bandari are a seafaring people hailing from the distant and now-lost country of [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md), somewhere far to the west of Nuitai. Two centuries ago, their fleets of broad-hulled ships bearing the sun-mark of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) crossed the endless ocean, bringing luxury goods, sophisticated craftsmanship, clothing, cuisine, culture, and organized religion that forever altered the island peoples circling [The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md) .

They settled chiefly in the western seas of Nuitai, on the [Matahari Archipelago](../location/matahari-archipelago.md), founding the radiant city of [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md), their golden beacon of faith and trade. Many also settled in the shadows of the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md), south of Matahari, where the lawful Zealots of Bandar have less influence.

They are known for their artistry, cosmopolitan culture, spiritual devotion to [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md), and seafaring skill. Bandari architecture and clothing favor gold, white, and deep reds; their cities are full of canals, prayer banners, and the scent of burning resin.

The spoken language of the Bandari, [Bandhasa](../language/bandhasa.md), is written using a script characterized by curvy flowing letters punctuated by loops. [Nuitai’lo](../language/nuitailo.md), the language of the native [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md), had no written script before the arrival of the Bandari, relying on oral tradition, and so they the native people adopted this imported script for themselves.

## Appearance and Varieties

There are two primary lineages among the Bandari: **Manusia **and **Vanara**. Though they share ancestry and culture, the two diverged in form and temperament over centuries.

### Manusia

The Manusia are the more common lineage among the Bandari, appearing broadly human yet bearing the indelible mark of their mythic ancestry. They are not too different in appearance from the native [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md), though they lack the imposing height and goliath build of the latter.

Their features are vigorous and striking. Their skin tends toward lighter shades which, unlike that of the Nuitagata, is not traditionally adorned with tattoos. Eye colors range from deep bronze to molten gold. Their hair, in shades of brown, gold, or black, grows full and wild like dancing flames, often dark at the roots and sun-lightened at the tips.

Bushy eyebrows and heavy sideburns frame their faces. Their brows, thick and expressive, often arch or curl in gentle waves reminiscent of stylized sunrays or the rolling motion of fire, lending their faces a vivid, almost luminous intensity.

Despite this, the Manusia take great care to keep their hair neatly trimmed or bound for the sake of appearances. Their overall look is one of restrained vitality, reflecting the dignity and discipline of a people renowned for their learning, order, and faith.

### Vanara

Sometimes known as the "Children of the Forest," the rarer Vanara Bandari share much of the same vivid appearance as their Manusia kin. They have the same bold stylish brows, thick sideburns and wild flame like hair with sun-warmed hues that hint at their radiant ancestry. Yet where the Manusia are defined by discipline and poise, the Vanara embody motion and grace.

Smaller and lighter than their Manusia cousins but just as devout and intelligent, Vanara are agile and quick, capable of feats of balance and speed unmatched by other peoples, only the amphibian [Ranagata](../species/anura.md) of the [Lanuroan](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md) rainforests rival their skill for acrobatics. Dense gold and brown fur covers their entire body. They have long prehensile tails, strong enough to hang from tree branches or the rafters and balconies of crowded cities. Their athleticism makes them more naturally talented at channeling [Kolindala](../law/mana.md). Vanara are celebrated as skilled martial artists, spies, and scouts. They are common among the ranks of the [Penyamun](../organization/the-penyamun.md).

Although Vanara sometimes face discrimination from the Manusia, stereotyped as thieves, both groups generally coexist peacefully. Many believe that Vanara Bandari were more common in the past and have sacred ancestry. Some depictions of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) himself show him as small, humanoid figure with a furry tail, suggesting he may have been a Vanara himself. This same art tends to depict Shaka more as a warrior of the people or benevolent trickster rather than a serene sage.

### Other Bandari Peoples

In addition to the Manusia and Vanara, there are a variety of other races that trace their ancestry from Bandar and identify as Bandari but through cultural identity rather blood lineage. They are also the descendants of people who sailed to Nuitai two centures ago and have lost contact with Bandar. For this reason "Bandari" can either refer to the race or the civilization.

Many are broadly human in appearance, but lacking the distinctive stylish eyebrows and hairstyles of the Manusia. Others are feline, avian, reptilian, aquatic, simian, and even proboscidean.

Most follow the teachings of Shaka, but there are a variety of minority religion and spiritual traditions practices in Bandari territories as well, the most common of which predate Shaka but have the same cosmological foundations.

This variety of culture and religion can be more easily found in the urban areas of the Matahari islands, especially the cosmopolitan Kota Emas where the Shakan faith takes center stage but minority religions are tolerated. This diversity is also common in lawless underworld of the Bayang Seas. Pirate crews are by far the most diverse microcosms of the people of Nuitai, where ancestry is not considered relevant.

## Society and Culture

Bandari life revolves around faith, craft, and discipline. The Teachings of Shaka guide moral conduct, shaping everything from architecture to cuisine.

Meals are rich with spice and balance. Rice is a common staple steamed in coconut milk, grilled fish wrapped in banana leaves, and sweet pandan cakes offered at shrines. These popular foods can be found in lively market stalls along the canals of Kota Emas, along with various fruits and vegetables such as breadfruit and star fruit. Durians are banned due to their smell.

The Zealots of Bandar are famous for their martial art, [Sila](../technology/sila.md), blending swordsmanship with meditative movement, reflecting both physical mastery and spiritual clarity. In all things, the Bandari prize refinement, learning, and devotion. They brought modern science, philosophy, literature and scholarship to Nuitai. They are a people as polished and enduring as the golden temples of Kota Emas.

## Technology and Craftsmanship

The Bandari are master shipwrights, builders, and artisans. Their broad-hulled jong and narrow lancaran ships are famed across the Maelstrom for their speed and beauty, built from hardwood and lacquered in red, black, and gold.

Bandari cities are threaded with canals, bridges, and tiered temples of stone and stucco, adorned with sun motifs and polished brass reliefs. Skilled metalworkers and weavers, they produce fine kris blades, intricate jewelry, and patterned silk bandanas and sarongs that shimmer like water in sunlight.
