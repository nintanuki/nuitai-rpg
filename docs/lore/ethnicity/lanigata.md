# Lanigata

Scattered across the islands of Nuitai are the ruins, temples and statues created by the "sky people," the Lanigata. They vanished four thousand years ago and the only traces left of them are their architecture and magical technology which is still active. The Lanigata discovered how to harness [Mana](../law/mana.md) to carve glyphs into stone, allowing them to move stone (elevators, doors), preserve memory, or animate constructs.

The Nuitagata avoid the stone megaliths, for they sometimes awaken and attack anyone who ventures too near. The only warning that stone will move is when the strange symbols carved into the stone begin to hum and glow. The symbols alone were mysterious to a people who until foreign contact recorded and preserved knowledge through oral tradition. The sky is filled with floating islands, the largest of which above a colossal eternal maelstrom is [The Floating Continent](../location/the-floating-continent.md) and the silhouettes of mysterious serpentine beings can be seen flying high above the clouds. Occasionally artifacts or stone golems are known to come falling from the sky. These falling golems are a threat if they are not destroyed at the fall. Some of these giants can be seen on the horizon, walking waist deep in the ocean, a humbling site in addition to the occasional sea beast that pops its head out in the distance.
