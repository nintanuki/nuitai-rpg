# Kota Emas

To the west of the great maelstrom, extending one thousand miles from the north of the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md) and to the south of the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) in the [Calm Ring](../article/sailing.md), is the Matahari Archipelago. The main island is on the famed Matahari Atoll, surrounded by a lagoon and a barrier reef. Here can be found the largest and most diverse city in all of Nuitai, Kota Emas, the central hub of trade and Shaka worship, home of the Luminary. However there is a wide variety of people of all backgrounds and faiths living in this city, including strange races and peoples from all over the endless ocean that the natives of Nuitai could only dream of. The sounds of strange music and the smell of spices fill the air. Vendors with stalls parked on the edges of river roads sell breadfruit, star fruit and bowls of hot rice.

The most advanced architecture, technology and hundreds of advanced ships that sail in and out of the lagoon are a stark contrast to the humble quiet life of the islands east of the maelstrom. The largest and main temple to Shaka in Nuitai can be found here, as can the headquarters of the archeological society dedicated to investigating the ruins of the sky people, and a criminal underworld hidden in the back alleys and under the streets of the city. In Kota Emas, the main roads are essentially man-made rivers that must be traveled by small boats.

Members of the law arm of the temple of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) train in barracks outside the city. The Zealots of [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md) are the warrior-priests of Shaka and keepers of order in Kota Emas, sworn to both uphold and defend the way of light; they root out injustice at the point of a blade. Far from mere soldiers, they are a living embodiment of devotion, their martial discipline woven into their worship. Masters of Sila, the blade based martial art perfected in the Bandari peninsula to the west, they strike with speed and precision while bolstering allies and breaking enemy ranks. They are a stark contrast to the pacifistic Shaka worshiping nature priests of Mara Iti.

Perhaps the most striking feature of the Matahari Atoll, is the gold and white guardian beast that can be found flying in the skies above the city of Kota Emas; [Ringanaga](../person/ringanaga.md).
