# Māra Priest

[The Nature Priests of Māra Iti](../organization/the-nature-priests-of-māra-iti.md) practice a syncretic religion that combines [Anúic](../organization/anúa.md) devotion to [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) with [Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md). They are pacifists who specialize in healing and protection by calling on the spirits of the forest and divine light through ritual dance.

**Elements: ****Lau **and **Ra**

**Roles: **Healing and Support

**Casting Method:** Ritual Dance

**Abilities:**

- Healing

- Cure status ailments

- Regen
