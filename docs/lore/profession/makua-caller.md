# Makua Caller

[The Callers](../organization/the-callers.md) are not just necromancers. They have a respectful relationship with the dead, and spirits are not summoned against their will, but come to the caller’s aid voluntarily. These spirits are usually Makua, the ancestors of the callers. Calling on the spirits of the underworld does not come without a price however; possession is always a risk. Using dark energies, callers can do a lot of damage, casting spells that terrify and debilitate their enemies. Their patron [Akua](../law/akua.md) is [Makei](../person/makei.md), who guides lost souls to [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md)'s drowned realm.

**Roles:** Magical attacker and Saboteur

**Casting Method: **Ritual Dance

**Augments: ****Mana ****→ Aku **magic damage

**Abilities:**

- Summon spirits from the [Underworld](../location/the-underworld.md)

- Lightning attacks

- Curses
