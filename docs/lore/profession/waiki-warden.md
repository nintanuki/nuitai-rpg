# Wai’Ki Warden

Wai’Ki Wardens, sentinels of the sacred springs, are masters of [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md); a defensive martial art developed in the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md), inspired by the flow and motion of water. They also have a connection to and familiarity of nature that gives them knowledge of healing. This combined with their defensive martial prowess makes them invaluable for keeping any party alive.

**Elements: Wai + Lau**

**Roles: **Healing and Defense

**Casting Method: **Martial Arts

**Abilities:**

- Defend while healing
