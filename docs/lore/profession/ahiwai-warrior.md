# Ahi’Wai Warrior

Ahi’Wai Warriors are masters of martial arts. They combine the fiery offense of [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) with the fluid defense of [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md) to become the ultimate physical fighters, changing stances when necessary. They are a strong matchup against enemies that rely on purely physical combat, as well as **Lau **and **Ahi **type enemies.

**Elements:** **Ahi **and **Wai**

**Roles: **Physical Fighter and Defender

**Casting Method: **Martial Arts

**Augments: ****Ahi ****→ ****Wai **physical damage

**Abilities:**

- Counterattack

- Taunt/Provoke

- Burning Weapons
