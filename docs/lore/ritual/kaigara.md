# Kaigara

Kaigara, also known as "Shells", is a popular game invented by the slow moving [Kame](../species/kame.md) of the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md). It is played on a smooth stone surface with a grid drawn in chalk, where players put down black and white cowrie shells to try and capture their opponents shells. It is a game that requires strategy and patience. Some [Kame](../species/kame.md) have been known to play the same game for years.
