# Kong

Kong is the ancient [Sarpic](../organization/sarpa.md) deity of primal rage. He is an unusual Deva, unlike the other serene and luxuriously adorned multi-armed members of the pantheon that represent noble or human ideals such as love, bravery or music, Kong is simply a colossal ape like beast that represents pure, unadulterated rage. Besides the golden crown or helmet he wears on his head, which stands out among the rest of his image, If he dons any clothing at all it is a simple loincloth at best. He is normally depicted with his mouth open and fangs visible, roaring, whilst wielding a large tree trunk or some other large object too big for a mortal to carry one handed, emphasizing his size. Kong also has the most consistent artistic depiction among the Devas, there is very little difference in how he is depicted across cultures and centuries old art.

His image and nature are not the only oddities. Kong is not known to have any worshipers. There are no shrines or temples to him. If there were any in the past they have been lost with the route to [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md). He is normally invoked as a metaphor for anger, and the closest mortals come to devoting themselves to Kong is invoking his name when they need pure physical strength, whether lifting a heavy object or fighting a tough battle, one might imagine themselves as Kong in that very moment.

While Kong has no devotees, even among the Sarpic minority, he is still a popular figure in [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) culture in his own way. He is afforded a morbid fascination and he commonly appears in stories, plays, art and as a festival mask for children. He is especially popular among children's stories and games.

Considering how alien Kong is compared to the rest of the Sarpic pantheon, and how simple and unvaried his image is, some historians have theorized that he is an outside deity that was introduced from a foreign culture, or perhaps Kong was a real animal that existed in Bandar, and much like the sacred guardian beasts of Nuitai was ancient and massive enough to be venerated as a god. It's possible Kong still roams the jungles of Bandar, but no one will ever know.
