# Aurora the Fire Dancer

Met in the fire islands. Very intense disposition, unapproachable personality, devout follower of Mauga. No sense of humor or tact. Speaks loudly and dramatically. She has heterochromatic eyes of fiery red and mystic purple. She uses [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) in combat, a fire based acrobatic martial art.

Her elements are **Ahi **and **Mana**, making her the ultimate dealer of physical and magical offense.
