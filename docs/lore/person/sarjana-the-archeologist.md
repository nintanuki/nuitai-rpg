# Sarjana the Archeologist

A bespectacled and curious young member of the archeological society, of [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) ancestry and accent, hailing from the Matahari Atoll but found exploring the ruins of [The Lanuroa Archipelago](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md) , or rather running for his life. Proof that bravery does not mean being afraid. Scans enemies to reveal weaknesses. Obsessed with the ruins of the [Sky People](../ethnicity/lanigata.md), his curiosity gets the group in trouble with an awakened stone golem. Analytical view of the world, more skeptical than superstitious.

His elements are **Wai **and **Ra**
