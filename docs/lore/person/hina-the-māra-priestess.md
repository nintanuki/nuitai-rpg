# Hina the Māra Priestess

Devout, proud, pious, ambitious, and a bit of a perfectionist. Hina is a young [Māra](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) priestess with strong religious convictions, to the annoyance of her peers. She is a devout follower of the teachings of Shaka. She puts on an air of serenity but is in fact easily provoked. She has to make a pilgrimage to [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md) at least once in her life and pray at the golden temple in order to become the high priestess of her village. Hina is a vegetarian, as is common among devotees of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md), and an only child. She has wavy black hair and lighter skin compared to her peers, with muted monochromatic golden brown eyes, evidence of her mixed Manusia [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) and [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md) ancestry.

Her elements are **Ra **and **Lau**.
