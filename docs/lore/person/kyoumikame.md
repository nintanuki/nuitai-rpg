# Kyoumikame

One of the six sacred beasts of Nuitai, considered to be a living Kamekami. Kyoumikame is the most ancient and colossal of the [Dai-Kame](../species/dai-kame.md). The size of an island, with an ecosystem on it's back, it patrols the seas of the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) and is believed to be it's protector by the native [Kame](../species/kame.md). Kyoumikame embodies the spirit of **Wai **(water).
