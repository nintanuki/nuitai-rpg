# Mauga

[Akua](../law/akua.md) of fire, volcanoes, the earth and the creation of the islands. Her domains are passion, destruction, creation, will, change, transformation, and renewal. Tribute is paid to Mauga on islands that have volcanoes, her wrath is placated. But on [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md) in particular her followers express love and devotion to her.

When Mauga stirs beneath the earth, the islands tremble. Her restlessness or anger causes the ground to quake, the mountains to breathe fire, and the sea itself to rise. The great waves that follow, tsunamis, are the result of her power shaking [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md)’s realm until his waters surge in answer.
