# PuaLele

When sailing the islands of [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md), it is impossible to miss the most extraordinary and enigmatic of its inhabitants: the islands’ ancient guardian, a colossal lepidopteran. Its vast, powerful yet delicate wings, colorful and translucent, shimmer in the sunlight. Its compound eyes give it an otherworldly, intelligent gaze. Long, slender legs extending beneath its body, and graceful antennae atop its crown, appear fragile in contrast to their immense size. As it soars gracefully through the skies, it casts reflections of vibrant rainbow light upon the waters below.

Occasionally, it pauses to perch atop the highest mountain, the gentle flapping of its wings sending a soothing breeze to the villagers who dwell beneath, while it emits a hauntingly beautiful cry. Its song is loud, echoing, and otherworldly, yet somehow sweet and tender, and can be heard for miles.

Pua'Lele is one of the six great sacred beasts of Nuitai, representing **Lau **(Nature)
