# Kiki the Witch Doctor

Met in [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md), Kiki is young and spunky but also morbid and inappropriate. She has extensive knowledge of ingredients and their effects in potion making, resulting in stained fingers and a strange lingering spell. She unintentionally has caused a fair number of explosions and dangerous effects from her alchemical experimentations. Kiki gets along with [Kailo](../person/kailo-the-wandering-chef.md) due to their shared youthful enthusiasm and sense of humor, although her darker sense of humor scares him sometimes. She looks up to [Tawiri](../person/tawiri-the-elderly-caller.md).

Despite having grown up in [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md) among native Nuitai, Kiki is of Vanara [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) ancestry. Being the only one among her peers with furry eyebrows, sideburns and a tail always made her feel like an outsider. She does not believe in the teachings of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) or the Doctrine of Recurrence. Kiki is a vegetarian.

Kiki's elements are **Lau **and **Aku**, making her a master of healing potions and debilitating poisons.
