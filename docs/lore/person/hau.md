# Hau

Among the many Akua Iti of Nuitai, Hau embodies the element of air and wind. As a being born from the powers of the three great Akua Nui, Hau exists in multiple temperaments or reflections, each tied to one of their domains.

- **Hau **[Lanu](../person/lanu.md): the gentle breeze that rustles the treetop leaves.

- **Hau **[Mauga](../person/mauga.md): the swirling hot air and rising smoke of fire and volcanoes.

- **Hau **[Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md): the gales and currents of the ocean that guide or destroy ships.

These forms are sometimes regarded as distinct sibling entities, other times as shifting moods of a single divine spirit, all named Hau.
