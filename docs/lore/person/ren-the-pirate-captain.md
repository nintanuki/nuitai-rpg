# Ren the Pirate Captain

Kirasame Ren is the captain of the Blood Lotus pirate crew. Known as the Killer Shark, she has a ruthless reputation and her name is feared throughout the [Bayang Seas](../location/bayang-seas.md). She has mastered the art of [Sila](../technology/sila.md), Bandari swordplay, and has both an indomitable defense and deadly offense at close range. The greatest swordsmen have fallen to her blade, but not always the result of a fair fight. She is not above using dirty tricks, a concealed pistol here, a poison tipped dagger there. Ren doesn't care about sportsmanship, she goes in for the kill with no mercy. She is a master of weapons, her most prized is a trident from the depths, although she rarely uses it in combat.

The Blood Lotus pirates are a diverse crew, anyone is welcome as long as they respect [the code](../ritual/undang-laut.md), the captain and the crew. They just need to pull their weight and keep up. Because they will allow anyone into their band the Blood Lotus are almost a microcosm of Nuitai itself. [Ikagata](../species/ikagata.md) mostly fill their ranks, but there are crew members with ancestry and backgrounds from all over Nuitai, mostly outcasts from their respective tribes for one reason or another. Kirasame Ren inspires fierce loyalty. She is a no-nonsense captain with little patience, but she is brave, decisive, a brilliant strategist, and most of all she is fair.

Ren herself is of mixed ancestry; her mother was a Manusia [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md), though she has no love for [Shaka](../person/shaka.md), and her father was an Ikagata. She has selachian features; pale skin, gills and rows of razor sharp teeth that can chew through steel. Her smile is more terrifying than her scowl. It is rumored that her father was one among the dreaded and inscrutable [Manogata](../species/manogata.md) that terrorize the seas of Nuitai and inspire the nightmares of children, although no one would dare suggest that to her face if they want to keep their skin on. She has soul-piercing red eyes and wild black hair.

The party meets Ren in the Bayang Seas and gets a ship upgrade when they meet her. Her elements are **Wai **and **Aku**, making her a defensive saboteur. She can use all kinds of weapons in battle including swords, guns and poison. Her ikagata ancestry makes her an excellent swimmer.
