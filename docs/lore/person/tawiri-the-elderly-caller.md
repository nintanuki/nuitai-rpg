# Tawiri the Elderly Caller

The third character to join the party. Hailing from the gloomy Makua Archipelago, Tawiri is an old woman and caller of the undead with a cynical but morbidly humorous disposition, to Hina’s chagrin.

She is world wary and has lived too long in the company of the dead. She is wrinkled and hunched over from age, with heterochromatic eyes of dark grey and violet, and wields a magic staff that doubles as a walking stick, adorned with ceremonial feathers and skulls.

Tawiri's elements are **Mana **and **Aku**.
