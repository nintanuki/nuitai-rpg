# Pōhaku Nui

The largest known Pōhaku, the great stone guardian Pōhaku Nui, is a colossal being taller than any mountain, which wades through the waters surrounding the [The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md). It takes an entire year for Pōhaku Nui to complete a circuit, moving with slow, deliberate care as though mindful not to disturb the sea, one misstep could unleash a tsunami. At times, it pauses mid-journey to gaze longingly at the floating continent above.

Pōhaku Nui is one of the six sacred beasts of Nuitai, representing **Spirit **([Mana](../law/mana.md)).
