# Kailo the Wandering Chef

Kailo hails from a small village in the [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) islands. He is a young cook with long, tied-back red hair, tan skin, heterochromatic pupils of ruby red and sapphire blue, and a strong physique. He radiates a warm, optimistic energy that draws people to him. Beneath this sunny exterior, however, he quietly wrestles with anxiety and self-doubt.

His father left when Kailo was young to explore the vast ocean of Nuitai, and Kailo idolizes him, speaking of him as if he were an epic legend. Inspired, Kailo dreams of exploring the world himself. However he cannot leave [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) as he has many younger siblings that he looks after. Kailo is a native [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md).

Empathetic and attentive, Kailo has a knack for connecting with others; strangers often confide in him while he cooks them a meal. Wasting food deeply upsets him, sometimes to the point of anger or melancholy. He travels between islands on his small boat, selling food and honing his skills in both cuisine and martial arts in his free time.

In combat, Kailo begins by creatively improvising, using kitchen utensils such as knives, rolling pins, and pot lids as weapons and shields. On his adventures he learns [Lelua](../technology/lelua.md) from [Aurora](../person/aurora-the-fire-dancer.md) and [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md) from [Gen'emon](../person/genemon-the-spring-guardian.md), eventually merging them into his own unique martial art.

Kailo’s journey begins when he meets [Hina](../person/hina-the-māra-priestess.md), a young nature priestess of [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md), and [Tawiri](../person/tawiri-the-elderly-caller.md) the elderly caller of the dead from [The Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md). Together with friends, he embarks on an adventure across Nuitai, as part of a growing party, clockwise around [The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md).

Kailo's elements are **Ahi **and **Wai**, making him a master martial artist with the perfect balance of both offense and defense.
