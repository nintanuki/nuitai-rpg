# Moanaloa

A colossal abyssal eel that patrols the dark haunted waters of the Makua Archipelago. It hides in submerged caves and labyrinthine chasms, the tips of its ghostly bioluminescent scales flickering like ethereal lights as it winds silently through the depths.

Moanaloa is one of the six great sacred beasts of Nuitai, the guardian and apex predator of the Makua Archipelago, and the natural embodiment of **Aku **(Shadow).
