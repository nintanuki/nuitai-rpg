# Paku the Navigator

Paku is an experienced sailor and master chef, with greying red hair and a lifetime spent exploring the seas of Nuitai. He once braved the Arctic northern waters of [Tokelau](../location/tokelau---the-north-sea.md), surfed near the eye of [The Great Maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md), and lived to tell about it. He has unmatched knowledge of navigation, sailing, and survival at sea. Years ago, he left his wife and young son to pursue the call of adventure, driven by a desire to explore the world and seek new challenges.

Bold and larger-than-life, Paku laughs in the face of danger and seems undaunted by any challenge. His boisterous nature and hearty appetite for food and drink can make him appear reckless or callous at times, but those who know him understand his courage and good humor run deep.

Skilled in [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md), which he learned during his travels to the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md). Paku has also mastered channeling [Mana](../law/mana.md) to cast powerful water elemental spells. When the party meets him, he provides a larger ship, serving as both guide and mentor on their oceanic journey.

His elements are **Wai **and **Mana**, making him proficient in physical defense and magical offense.
