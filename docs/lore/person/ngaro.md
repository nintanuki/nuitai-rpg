# Ngaro

An aspect of [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md), the princess of secrets, N’garo is the goddess of the hidden, the unseen and the forbidden. Where Aku embodies the instinctive fear of darkness, N’garo governs what is actually concealed inside it. She moves and hides within [Aku](../person/aku.md)’s shadow, using the cover he creates to obscure objects, knowledge and intentions. She is the patron saint of those who seek arcane and forbidden knowledge.

She is also tied to the loss or distortion of information. Forgetfulness, misplaced memories, and even deliberate lies are considered part of her influence. Anything that slips from the grasp of understanding, whether by intention or accident, falls under N’garo’s domain.

N’garo is depicted as a woman in rags whose age shifts between youth and old age. Her posture is slightly hunched, as if she’s always leaning toward something unseen. She often wrings her hands, her creepy smile sits unevenly on her face, suggesting she knows more than she shares. Her eyes look tired, with dark circles beneath them, and her hair is usually shown loose and unkempt. The overall impression is of someone who spends her time in places where light and attention rarely reach.
