# Moahi

Mo'ahi is a giant red salamander that makes its home within the largest volcano on the biggest island of [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md). It occasionally emerges to crawl slowly along the volcano’s slopes, and only rarely descends into the jungle. When it does, every step reshapes the environment, leaving fire-scorched terrain and molten rivers in its wake. Mo’ahi is deliberate and slow-moving, yet an unstoppable force of nature.

One of the six great sacred beasts of Nuitai, embodying the spirit of **Ahi **(Fire).
