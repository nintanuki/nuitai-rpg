# Matahari

Matahari is a [Sarpic](../organization/sarpa.md) deity, the ancient [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) god of the sun and it's light. In early Sarpic tradition he was regarded as a literal solar deity, but in later centuries his domain came to be understood more philosophically as law, order, and truth, illuminating and burning away deceit and illusion.

When [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md) became the dominant religion of [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md), [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) himself was said to have either mantled Matahari or was in fact Matahari in mortal form, depending on the interpretation. When this happened "Shaka as Matahari" gained the additional themes of wisdom, compassion and wil. Matahari is an old epithet for Shaka and is not commonly used in this day and age, but the [Matahari Archipelago](../location/matahari-archipelago.md) was mostly likely named after "Shaka as Matahari" since the islands were discovered when the Shakan faith was already eight hundred years old.

A small number of Sarpic practitioners continue to venerate Matahari in his older, pre-Shakan form.
