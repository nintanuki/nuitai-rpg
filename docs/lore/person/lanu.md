# Lanu

[Akua](../law/akua.md) of the sun, the sky and the forests. Her domain is the energies of all living things, embodying life, light, growth, harmony, and the gentle rain. With the spread of [Shaka](../person/shaka.md)’s teachings among the people of [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) and [The Lanuroa Archipelago](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md), the aspects of the sun and light traditionally associated with Lanu came to be attributed to him, to the chagrin of the [Maki Sages](../organization/maki-sages.md).
