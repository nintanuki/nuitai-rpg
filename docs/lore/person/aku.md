# Aku

An aspect of [Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md), the King of Shadows, Aku is the personification of darkness and fear. He exists in those spaces that light does not reach, in the shadows of trees, the pitch black in caves, and in the dead of a moonless night Aku's body grows. As he is shadow personified, he cannot exist directly in the path of [Ra Lanu](../person/ra-lanu.md)'s light, but is defined in absence of it. [N'garo](../person/ngaro.md), the princess of secrets, hides in his shadow.

Aku represents not just darkness but also the fear of it. The hair standing on the back of your neck when you hear a rustling in the bushes in the dead of night, that is Aku at play. It should be noted that Aku is not evil, fear of the dark and the unknown is a natural instinct that can keep you alive. Aku fear aspect is a natural warning.

He is depicted the least like a human among the Akua Iti, as if something from a child's nightmare. His fearsome visage a broad, rectangular mask twisted into a permanent snarl. Angry eyes glare from deep-set sockets, and jagged horns jut from either side of his head like the carved flanges of a ceremonial effigy. His mouth gapes wide beneath them, crowded with too many razor sharp teeth pressing against a space too small to hold them. Aku masks modeled after this fearsome countenance are popular among the children of the [Makua Archipelago](../location/the-makua-archipelago.md) during festivals, casting exaggerated shadows as they dance in front of torches and lanterns.

The fact that Aku is depicted as having fiery eyebrows similar to the [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) race has not gone unnoticed, however this depiction predates the arrival of the Bandari in Nuitai for centuries.
