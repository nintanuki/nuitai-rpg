# Maika the Maki Sage

Maika is a young [Maki Sage](../organization/maki-sages.md) the party first meets in [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) but does not join until later. He has tan skin, with heterochromatic pupils of earthy green and deep purple, and uniquely white hair. Maika is well spoken with a wise, outwardly calm demeanor and seems to be unusually worldly for his age. Maika can be acerbic at times, lacking tact in how he speaks to others, and is a bit arrogant and opinionated. But he can admit when he is wrong, often apologizing or chastising himself for unsagely behavior. It can be difficult to tell when he is sincere, or if he deliberately gets on [Hina](../person/hina-the-māra-priestess.md)'s nerves. Despite his outward show of being above worldly pleasures, he enjoys flirting, drinking, and other indulgences, often brushing them off with clever, self-assured words that make even his mischief sound like philosophy. Maika is a vegetarian, as is common among devotees of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md). He has a sister that he does not get along with.

His elements are **Lau **and **Mana**, making him an excellent healer and offensive spellcaster of nature elemental magic.
