# Wai

Wai is the [Akua](../law/akua.md) Iti of water. He exists in multiple temperaments or reflections, each tied to one of domains of the three great Akua Nui:

- Wai [Lanu](../person/lanu.md) is fresh water and the gentle nourishing rain, the life giving liquid of the islands of Nuitai.

- **Wai **[Moanaku](../person/moanaku.md) is salt water, the substance and spray of the ocean waves.

- **Wai **[Mauga](../person/mauga.md) can be found in the hotsprings and geysers of [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md).

There is some ambiguity and variation between the islands as to how to interpret Wai's domain regarding blood. While blood is the liquid life force of living organic things, traditionally Lanu's domain, blood also represents the red hot passion that Mauga is known for, and so blood is sometimes referred to as Wai Lanu or Wai Mauga, depending on the intended symbolism and meaning, blood as life or blood as passion.
