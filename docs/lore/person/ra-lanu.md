# Ra Lanu

Ra, or La as he is called in the south, is the Akua Iti of the sun and it's light. He is traditionally considered an aspect of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md), and associated with the nourishing light that allows her vegetation to grow.

In the last century, the influence of [Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) missionaries and their preaching of [The Teachings of Shaka](../organization/the-teachings-of-shaka.md) in Nuitai has lead many Nuitagata in the east to equate [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) and his sun symbolism with Ra. This also re-contextualized the association of sunlight with wisdom and moral purity.

This identification of the foreign sun god Shaka with the native sun god Ra has been clarified and formalized in the doctrine of [The Nature Priests of Māra Iti](../organization/the-nature-priests-of-māra-iti.md), and wholly rejected by the [Maki Sages](../organization/maki-sages.md), as well as the Nuitagata of the south who never accepted the Bandari faith.

Outside of Māra, many eastern Nuitagata still regard Shaka and Ra as distinct beings, and simply follow Shaka’s teachings alongside their native faith without merging the two.

Ra [Mauga](../person/mauga.md), or La Mauga as she is known in the [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md) to the southeast, is the personification of firelight.

*Kameterasu *is the native sun god of the [Kame](../species/kame.md) people, he is the Kame analog of Ra Lanu and was later identified with [Shaka](../person/shaka.md) in syncretic [Wai'Ki](../organization/waiki.md) practices.
