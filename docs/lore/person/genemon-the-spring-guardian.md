# Genemon the Spring Guardian

A member of the [Kame](../species/kame.md) race, hailing from the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) and the first non-human [Kailo](../person/kailo-the-wandering-chef.md) or [Hina](../person/hina-the-māra-priestess.md) have ever met. Gen'emon is an old man of a turtle, even for Kame years, he is a master of [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md) and healing medicine. He and the [caller](../person/tawiri-the-elderly-caller.md) get along due to their shared years of wisdom, even if they disagree sometimes.
