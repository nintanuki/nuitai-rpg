# Wai’Kenpō

Inspired by the flow and motion of water. Wai’Kenpō, or "The Way of the Water Fist" is a famous martial art, with a philosophy of self defense over attack, and has spread its influence throughout Nuitai, however none are as proficient at it as the [Kame](../species/kame.md) themselves, and only spellcasters who harness the power of [Mana](../law/mana.md) can hope to break the defense of their most skilled martial artists. Wai’Kenpō was developed by devout practitioners of [Wai'Ki](../organization/waiki.md).
