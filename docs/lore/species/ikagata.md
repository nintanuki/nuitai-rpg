# Ikagata

The Ikagata are the native intelligent inhabitants of the ocean under the surface. Also known as "the fish people", the term is incredibly broad and as the ikagata themselves would point out, is about as specific as referring to all the races and peoples that walk on land as "land people." There is an incredible genetic and cultural diversity of civilizations under the ocean, entire cities and countries hidden beneath the waves, but for those who can't breath underwater it is a world they will never see. The ikagata that live in the waters to the northwest of the [maelstrom](../location/the-great-maelstrom.md), around the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) are commonly squid people. Interestingly enough the word "ika" in the [Kame](../species/kame.md) language means squid.
