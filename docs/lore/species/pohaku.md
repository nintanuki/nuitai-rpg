# Pohaku

The sky people, the [Lanigata](../ethnicity/lanigata.md), left traces of their existence all across Nuitai. The most astonishing relics of their advanced civilization are the animated stone golems, known to the people of Nuitai as Pohaku, that still guard the ancient temples.

They vary in size, from towering humanoids to mountains that move. Some patrol specific areas, awakening only when something approaches; others are eternally dormant or wander aimlessly without purpose.

How the Pohaku move remains a mystery. Scholars believe it has something to do with the glowing glyphs carved into their stone bodies. Somehow, the [Lanigata](../ethnicity/lanigata.md) discovered how to imbue [Mana](../law/mana.md) into stone, giving it life. Yet the Pohaku are over three thousand years old, and some believe the [Mana](../law/mana.md), or the souls, use to animate them are now trapped, unable to be reborn or move on.

The largest known Pohaku is the great stone guardian [Pōhaku Nui](../person/pōhaku-nui.md)
