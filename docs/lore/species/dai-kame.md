# Dai-Kame

It is debatable whether the chelonians or the Kame themselves are related to or descended from the massive gentle sea turtles that travel the waters of Nuitai, some so large as to be mistaken for islands themselves. Indeed some are so big and slow that flora and fauna have had time to grow on the backs of their shells. These giant turtles, sometimes known as Dai-Kame, are more common among the waters of the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md) and are respected and protected by the Kame themselves.
