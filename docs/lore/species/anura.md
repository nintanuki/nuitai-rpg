# Anura

The Anura are one of the many non-human intelligent species found throughout Nuitai. They are amphibious humanoids with large, bulging eyes atop their heads and a fondness for cuisine that primarily features insects. Known for their sharp intelligence and diverse regional forms, they come in two varieties.

### Ranagata

Small and agile, these amphibious natives inhabit the eastern and northeastern regions of Nuitai, particularly [Māra Iti](../location/māra-iti-islands.md) and the [Lanuroa Archipelago](../location/the-lanuroa-archipelago.md). They have bright colorful skin that is slippery to the touch, and large webbed feet that makes them graceful swimmers and natural acrobats. None rival the Ranagata passion for motion; diving contests, canopy races and daring leaps between treetop bridges are common pastimes.

Ranagata have long co-existed with the native [Nuitagata](../ethnicity/nuitagata.md) of the east and many practice [Anúic](../organization/anúa.md) traditions, typically as followers of [Lanu](../person/lanu.md). Fresh water is very important to the Ranagata, not just for drinking but for keeping their skin moist, and Lanu provides the gentle rain that nourishes the forests.

Ranagata typically build their homes in the high treetops of dense forests, with bridges connecting each structure creating floating villages in the canopies. Their native language is Rana'lo, characterized by rapid high pitched tonal ribbits that is a challenge for even the most skilled linguists to master.

### Kaerujin

Where the Ranagata thrive among the trees and rain of the east, the Kaerujin embody a more earthen strength. Most Kaerujin live in the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md), though many can also be found in [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md), where peoples of every kind intermingle.

They are typically larger than their Ranagata cousins, with dry bumpy skin, and are less acrobatic and slow moving, preferring to stay on the low earth than the treetops. They are more stocky and round than slim or sleek, and more muted in color. They could easily be mistaken for the [Kame](../species/kame.md) they live with if not for their lack of shells.

Despite their slower gait, they can be powerful warriors and a Kaerujin trained in [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md) can be surprisingly agile and formidable in combat. While many Kaerujin practice the Kame spiritual tradition of [Wai'Ki](../organization/waiki.md), they revere their own Anuran pantheon rather than the [chelonian](../species/chelonians.md) gods of the Kame. Kaerujin are naturally talented at long sessions of still, silent meditation, and have been mistaken for boulders during their practice.

The Kaerujin language, Kaerugo, is a sister language of Kamego. It shares the same traits of open vowels, a steady even flow of syllables, and intricate politeness levels of grammar, but punctuated by deep, slow and throaty croaking sounds.

[Bandari](../ethnicity/bandari.md) biologists have speculated that Anura of Ranagata and Kaerujin ancestries have evolved from or share a common ancestor with tree frogs and toads, respectively.
