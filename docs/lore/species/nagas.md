# Nagas

Sentient, serpent-like beings originally from [Bandar](../organization/bandar.md) , the Nagas now inhabit the cave systems of the Matahari Atoll and the sewers of [Kota Emas](../settlement/kota-emas.md). They are highly dangerous and aggressive toward outsiders; few who encounter them survive. Their cunning intelligence, venomous bite, and mastery of stealth make them formidable predators in both wilderness and urban environments.
