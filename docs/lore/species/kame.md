# Kame

The Kame are a [Chelonian](../species/chelonians.md) people who dwell within the [Kame Kingdom](../organization/kame-kingdom.md), a maritime realm nestled among the northwestern seas of Nuitai. Chelonians are a reptilian family of races with long necks and shells they can hide in. Among the chelonian kind, the Kame are the largest and most culturally developed, having cultivated language, medicine, and martial traditions unknown to their sea-dwelling kin. They are excellent swimmers, practitioners of medicine and martial artists, and have developed their own defensive form of martial art known as [Wai’Kenpō](../technology/waikenpō.md). The [Kame](../species/kame.md) practice their own form of Anuic beliefs known as [Wai'Ki](../organization/waiki.md). The Kame are pescatarians, that is, the only meat they eat comes from the sea. However some Kame who are especially devoted to the Kami of nature are full on vegetarians, eschewing even fish. It is believed that the Kame are descended from or distantly related to the [Dai-Kame](../species/dai-kame.md), the giant turtles that can be found roaming in the northwestern seas of Nuitai.
