# Nakahiahi

Giant red serpents that wind they way in and out of the volcanoes of [The Fire Islands](../location/the-fire-islands.md). The same serpents have been observed entering the crater of one island and emerging from the vents of others, suggesting there may be an extensive network of subterranean lava tubes and volcanic conduits linking the archipelago; a hidden plumbing of molten rock that the Nakahi'ahi use to traverse great distances beneath the islands’ scorched surface.
